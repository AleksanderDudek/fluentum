# Changelog

All notable changes to Fluentum. Dates are in the Europe/Warsaw timezone.

## Unreleased

### 1.0.0 — the first version

- **Conversations aloud:** sixteen everyday conversations, A1 to A2, in a messenger-style chat — English for Polish
  speakers, Polish for English speakers. The partner speaks first, aloud, with the meaning under each line; you answer
  aloud, with the pronunciation spelt your way and the meaning. Every line is checked as you speak.
- **Three ways, three stars:** read a conversation, then say it from prompts (first letters only), then from memory (only
  the meaning). Words appear as you say them; "Show the line" helps when you are stuck, but that run earns no star.
- **The course:** conversations as stepping stones by level, with their stars; the suggested next step brings each one back
  a little later to make it stick. Home starts it with one tap; the summary goes straight on to the next.
- **Grammar you can see:** subject, predicate, adjectives and objects coloured together with their translation (and
  underlined the Polish school way); tap a word to light its pair, open the word list, or turn the colours off.
- **Motivation:** points and 20 levels, 49 achievements (conversations, stars, mastered conversations, A1 and A2 complete,
  streaks, golden quarter-hour…), daily goal in lines, streak freezes, the golden quarter-hour, friends' leaderboard by
  link, shareable cards for the day and for a conversation.
- **Lori**, a lorikeet, guides you; pick one of eight characters as your avatar.
- **Private and free:** no accounts, no server, no ads; offline Whisper for fully on-device recognition; backups with an
  optional password, restore with undo; installable app that works offline.
- Started from the engine of Teleo (speech recognition, matcher, live listening, gamification, backups).
